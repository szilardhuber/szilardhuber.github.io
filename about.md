#### 2008 - 2010: Senior Software Engineer @ [LogMeIn](https://www.goto.com)

After the intense DSS Consulting experience, I joined LogMeIn's newly opened R&D center in Szeged as a Senior Software Engineer. This was an exciting opportunity to work on a globally-used product from my hometown.

LogMeIn (now GoTo) was founded in 2003 in Budapest and became a leading provider of remote access and collaboration software, serving millions of users worldwide with products like LogMeIn Pro and Join.me.

**Key Contributions:**
- Worked on the core screen capturing and remote control functionality that powered LogMeIn's flagship products
- Developed a bandwidth optimization solution that significantly reduced network traffic during common screen-sharing scenarios (moving windows, switching applications)
- Designed and implemented a rect-based caching system: split screen content into 16×16 pixel rectangles, calculated hashes for each, and maintained synchronized hashmaps on both client and server—transmitting only cache IDs instead of full pixel data when rectangles matched
- Added an offset-detection mechanism that checked if rectangles shifted by small amounts (indicating window movement) could be found in the cache, then extended this offset search across the entire screen when patterns matched—the CTO appreciated this approach for its simplicity and impact
- Began refactoring the screen capture logic into a dedicated module, establishing architectural foundations for future improvements

This role marked my transition into working on large-scale consumer software with performance-critical real-time requirements. The challenge of optimizing remote desktop performance taught me to think creatively about algorithmic solutions that could make measurable differences in user experience.
