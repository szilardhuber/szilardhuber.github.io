#### 2007 - 2008: Software Engineer @ [DSS Consulting](https://dss.hu)

A recruiter found me while I was at Graphisoft, offering an opportunity to lead and build a new development center in Szeged, my wife's hometown where we wanted to settle. After an intensive recruitment process including four rounds of interviews in less than a week—complete with a sports psychologist evaluation declaring me a "natural born leader"—I received an offer from the CEO.

DSS Consulting is a Hungarian IT consulting firm specializing in custom software development, data quality solutions, and digital transformation services for enterprise clients.

**The Crisis:** On my second day, the CTO (who had been on vacation during my hiring) took the CEO to lunch and announced his resignation—along with the *entire* development team. The Szeged office plans were immediately canceled. I became the company's sole developer with active projects and contractual commitments.

**Rising to the Challenge:**
- Took emergency ownership of a critical software development project for the Hungarian Financial Supervisory Authority
- Coordinated with a professional vendor team sourced by the CEO to ensure project continuity
- Successfully delivered the project on time despite having zero prior knowledge of the year-old codebase, preventing financial damages to the company
- Conducted training sessions for client employees on the delivered system
- Simultaneously recruited, hired, and onboarded a team of 4 developers within 6 months
- Integrated the new team without missing deadlines or impacting delivery velocity

This intense 8-month period was a crucible that forged my crisis management skills, customer interaction capabilities, and practical understanding of project leadership under extreme pressure. It proved that leadership isn't just about titles—it's about stepping up when everything falls apart.

#### 2004 - 2007: C++ Developer @ [Graphisoft](https://graphisoft.com)

I joined Graphisoft as part of the 2D team developing ArchiCAD, widely regarded as the best architecture CAD software at the time. Graphisoft had an exceptional development culture with mature engineering practices and a world-class product used by thousands of architects globally.

Graphisoft is the maker of Archicad, the industry-leading BIM (Building Information Modeling) software trusted by AEC professionals worldwide.

**Key Contributions:**
- Worked as part of the team that laid the architectural foundations for the new Teamwork functionality, which transformed ArchiCAD from a single-architect tool into a collaborative platform for large architecture firms designing major buildings
- Developed C++ components for 2D visualization (the product had separate teams for 2D and 3D rendering at the time)
- Gained cross-platform development experience, working with Mac architecture including handling big-endian vs. little-endian differences
- Took my first management role by leading a small vendor team for several months, overseeing their development efforts and acting as technical liaison

This role deepened my understanding of professional software packaging, shipping products to thousands of customers, and working in high-performing engineering teams. I also discovered that I enjoyed technical leadership alongside hands-on development.

#### 2002 - 2004: Software Engineer @ [PEC Corp](https://www.peccorp.com)

My journey in professional software development began at PEC Corp, where I was selected from over a hundred candidates in a competitive alumni-wide interview process based on C++ coding exercises. This achievement marked the start of my career in production-grade software development.

PEC Corp is a global leader in battery cell manufacturing and testing equipment with over 40 years of innovation in industrial automation.

**Key Contributions:**
- Developed integration modules for a factory line management system, connecting our C++ software with a third-party camera-based visual QA system
- Spent 2 months on-site at a manufacturing facility, working directly with production teams and vendor engineers to fine-tune the integration and adapt to specific factory requirements
- Gained foundational experience in industrial software development, learning how to build reliable systems for real-world manufacturing environments

This role taught me the importance of cross-vendor collaboration, on-site consulting, and adapting software to meet practical production needs in a multi-cultural organization.
