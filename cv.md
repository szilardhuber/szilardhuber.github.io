### 2014 - 2018: Software Developer @ Starschema

Returned to individual contributor role at Starschema after six years in engineering management at LogMeIn. Reconnected with a former direct report who had joined Starschema—in an amusing role reversal, he became my manager.

**VirtDB - Product Development:** Joined 3-person team building VirtDB, a product designed to make SAP data accessible for external data processing tools. Built technically solid, high-performance application handling large data volumes with flexibility. Despite strong technical foundations, struggled with product-market fit—our vision and go-to-market strategy didn't translate into sustainable client acquisition. Pivoted to consulting when product initiative wound down. Learned that technical excellence alone doesn't guarantee commercial success without proper distribution strategy and market validation.

**Tableau Performance Monitoring:** Joined Bay Area startup client after they lost their development team. Built Performance Monitoring and Optimization tool for Tableau Servers, leveraging Starschema's Tableau Zen Master expertise. System processed large volumes of Tableau internal data to track session usage and identify bottlenecks. Gained exposure to Fortune 500 companies including ExxonMobil, Apple, Shell, Tesla, SpaceX, Netflix, and Pepsi. Project ended when Tableau indicated they wouldn't recommend third-party tools to enterprise customers, eliminating our planned sales channel.

This period provided crucial lessons about product-market fit, the importance of distribution strategy, and the reality that technical strength must be paired with viable business models.
